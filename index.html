01 About

The short version

I like systems that hold up outside the classroom.

I am a BSc Information Technology student at the University of Energy and Natural Resources, based in Kumasi, Ghana. My focus is cloud engineering and IT support, with frontend development as the layer where the work becomes visible to people.

I started with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, then moved into cloud through AWS re/Start, where I worked with Linux, networking, security and core AWS services. I am AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner, and I have completed support internships at Christian Service University and I-Akrotech, testing applications and helping real users.

At UENR I served as Deputy Welfare Chairperson for the Department of IT and Decision Sciences, which taught me to listen, coordinate and follow through. The next step is an internship or junior role where I can support real systems and keep growing.

Currently finishing my BSc at UENR and building MedQueue GH, a healthcare access app piloted with UENR Health Services.
